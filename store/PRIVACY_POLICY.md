# Chute Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Chute is a local browser-to-desktop handoff tool. Its purpose is to let users intentionally move files, images, links, and selected text between webpages and a Chute companion running on their own computer.

## Data Chute handles

Chute may handle files, webpage images, links, selected text, filenames, MIME types, timestamps, local Chute IDs, capture source URLs, and Chute preference values only as needed to provide user-facing Chute features.

## Where data goes

Basket contents and browser captures are sent to the user's local Chute companion through the loopback address `127.0.0.1:17891` and stored on that computer. Chute does not operate a remote Chute cloud storage service for this basket.

## Chrome Sync preferences

The extension uses `chrome.storage.sync` for small preference values such as display settings, capture options, thumbnail settings, and browser access mode. If Chrome Sync is enabled, Google may synchronize those preference values. File contents, Chute history, and captured images are not placed in Chrome Sync by Chute.

## Website access

Chute runs on ordinary HTTP and HTTPS pages to provide its floating drop target, user-initiated drag/drop handling, image capture, right-click actions, and delivery of user-selected Chute files back into webpages. Page access is not used to build advertising profiles, sell browsing histories, or collect unrelated browsing activity.

## Local provenance

When the user captures a browser image, Chute may locally record the page URL, direct image URL, capture time, and local locations of artifacts created by that capture. These provenance records stay with the user's local Chute data unless the user moves or shares them.

## Payments

If a Chute desktop companion is purchased through a publisher checkout, payment details are handled by the disclosed payment provider. The browser extension does not store raw payment-card numbers in the Chute basket.

## Retention and deletion

Local Chute files and history remain on the user's computer until removed according to Chute's local storage behavior. Clearing the live basket does not necessarily erase dated history or preserved copies. Users control and can remove their local Chute data.

## Sharing

Chute does not sell user data, Chute basket contents, or browsing history. Chute does not transfer basket contents to third parties for advertising, credit decisions, or data resale.

## Contact

Use the publisher support/contact information shown on the Chrome Web Store listing or project website.
